Contemporary Tamil literature and the writers behind it

Tamil has one of the oldest continuous literary traditions in the world. This site is a starting point for exploring the novelists, poets and short-story writers who carry it forward today.

Writers to know

Six voices, from the Tamil renaissance to the present day. Each entry is a doorway, not a complete biography. Portraits are placeholder illustrations.

Subramania Bharati

சுப்பிரமணிய பாரதி · 1882–1921

Revolutionary poet of the Tamil renaissance, who wrote on freedom, women's equality and a modern Tamil voice.

Kuyil Pattu, Kannan Pattu, Panchali Sapatham

Puthumaipithan

புதுமைப்பித்தன் · 1906–1948

Pioneer of the modern Tamil short story, famed for his sharp satire and restless experiments with form.

Ponnagaram, Sabavimosanam

Ka. Na. Subramanyam

க. நா. சுப்ரமண்யம் · 1912–1988

Critic, poet and novelist who championed modernism and shaped how Tamil literature is judged.

Poithevu, Oru Naal

Sundara Ramaswamy

சுந்தர ராமசாமி · 1931–2005

Novelist, poet and founder of the journal Kalachuvadu, admired for his precise, ironic prose.

Oru Puliyamarathin Kathai, J.J. Silakurippugal

Asokamitran

அசோகமித்திரன் · 1931–2017

Chronicler of ordinary lives in Chennai and Secunderabad, writing with quiet humour and restraint.

Karaindha Nizhalgal, Thaneer

Jeyamohan

ஜெயமோகன் · b. 1962

Prolific novelist, short-story writer and essayist working across epic, realist and philosophical modes.

Vishnupuram, Kotravai

Recurring themes

  • Caste and social justice – Dalit literature has reshaped what Tamil fiction looks like and who writes it.
  • Gender and identity – feminist and queer voices in poetry and prose.
  • Land and language – regional dialects, agrarian life and the pull of the city.
  • Diaspora – Tamil writing from Sri Lanka, Singapore, Malaysia and beyond.

Where to start reading

Many of these writers are available in English translation. Good first steps: a Puthumaipithan short-story collection, Asokamitran's Water, or Sundara Ramaswamy's Story of a Tamarind Tree.
