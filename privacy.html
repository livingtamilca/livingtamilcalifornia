What we collect

When you donate, register for an event, or contact us, we collect the information you give us, such as your name, email, phone, mailing address and gift amount. Payments are processed by our donation platform; Living Tamil California does not see or store your full card or bank details.

We use your information to process gifts and registrations, send receipts, keep records required by law, and tell you about our programs. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
