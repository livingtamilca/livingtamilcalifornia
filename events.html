The Literary Circle

Our monthly gathering, held as the VLC Bay Area Chapter.

  • WhenSecond Saturday of every month · 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • WhereFremont Main Library, 2400 Stevenson Blvd, Fremont, CA 94538
  • NextSecond Saturday of this month

An unhurried morning of reading and conversation. Each month we take up a pre-announced selection, usually three or four short stories or a short novel, drawn mostly from modern Tamil literature, with classic and modern world literature woven in.

  1. வாசிப்புRead aheadThe stories are shared in advance so everyone comes having read them.
  2. உரைA speaker for each storyOne member introduces each work: its writer, its world and what it stirred in them.
  3. உரையாடல்Open discussionThe circle opens up and everyone joins the conversation.

உரையாடல் தமிழில் · Conversations are held in Tamil.

Everyone is welcome: come as a reader, stay as a friend.
